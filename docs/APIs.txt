APIs


GET /accounts: returns all accounts from the accounts table, including risk level and premium.
GET /accounts/{id}/brief: the page payload. It bundles account info, the snapshot, milestones, commitments, risks, opportunities, contradictions and the latest brief text.
GET /accounts/{id}/policies: returns policies associated with the account.
GET /accounts/{id}/claims, /accounts/{id}/service-cases, /accounts/{id}/documents: the tab views.
