## Two-minute business-value statement

**0:00–0:30 — Problem.** Producers enter renewal conversations with records spread across
policy, claim, service, billing and relationship sources. A missed commitment, unresolved
claim or date contradiction can undermine a valuable client discussion.

**0:30–1:00 — Solution.** Meridian brings the permitted facts and evidence into one concise
workspace. It highlights which renewal needs attention, prepares an agenda and separates
verified facts from opportunity hypotheses. The human sees confidence, age and contradictions.

**1:00–1:30 — Control.** Model output must pass structured and completeness checks; fallback
remains usable and labeled. Access rules apply before context reaches the model. A producer
reviews proposed wording and explicitly decides next actions. The system never binds or contacts.

**1:30–2:00 — Value to validate.** The expected benefit is faster preparation and more reliable
follow-through, leading to better conversations. We have not proved retention or revenue lift.
A pilot should time manual versus assisted preparation, review omission/citation rates, monitor
action follow-through and measure model cost per accepted useful brief before claiming ROI.

## Known limitations

Two personas; synthetic integrations; screenshot-only executive agency metrics; local SQLite
and worker pool; no distributed queue or production deployment; runtime briefs/jobs reset at
startup; new workflows not fully regression verified; automated accessibility is not certification;
no production business outcome measurements; incomplete historical SDLC prompt retention.
