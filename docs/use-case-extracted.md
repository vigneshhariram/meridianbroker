User Story 
Strengthen Client Retention and Producer Effectiveness 
Underlying solution: Producer and Client Intelligence Copilot for Meridian Brokerage 
Group  
Meridian’s growth depends on retaining client relationships, enabling producers and account teams, and identifying 
responsible opportunities to deepen those relationships. Yet client, policy, claims, billing, service, activity and 
document information is distributed across multiple systems and acquired agencies. Producers may spend valuable 
time assembling context and can enter renewal or stewardship meetings without a complete view of commitments, 
risk or opportunity. 
As a Meridian producer or account executive, I want a concise, evidence -grounded client brief before a renewal or 
stewardship meeting, so that I can focus the conversation on the client’s priorities, address service or renewal risks 
early, fulfil open commitments and identify appropriate coverage or relationship opportunities without unsupported 
recommendations. 
The experience should improve preparation and decision quality, not replace producer judgement. Every risk or opportunity 
must show supporting evidence, recency, confidence, assumptions and any contradiction that requires human review.  
Expanded Details 
• Client 360 Assembly: Combine synthetic account, policy, claims, activity, service, billing and document data into a 
traceable profile. 
• Meeting Preparation: Generate a one-page brief with relationship summary, upcoming milestones, open 
commitments, recent changes and suggested discussion points. 
• Renewal Risk Detection: Identify late exposure information, open claims, service issues, unresolved endorsements, 
carrier constraints and approaching deadlines. 
• Coverage and Opportunity Signals: Surface evidence-based coverage gaps, product adjacency and referral 
opportunities while clearly separating facts from suggestions. 
• Persona Adaptation: Provide producer, account executive, service leader and regional leader views based on the same 
evidence. 
• Responsible Recommendations: Display evidence, confidence, assumptions and required human review; never bind 
coverage, alter policy data or contact a client autonomously.

Functional Scope 
• Synthetic client data hub: Accounts, contacts, policies, claims, activities, service cases and documents. 
• Client intelligence model: Relationship timeline, renewal milestones, issues, commitments and opportunity signals. 
• Brief generator: Executive summary, agenda, talking points, risks and follow-ups. 
• Evidence panel: Source record, recency, confidence and contradiction handling. 
• Conversational copilot: Grounded questions, drill-down and persona-sensitive answers. 
• Controlled next actions: Draft follow-up, create review task or prepare referral subject to approval. 
• Feedback and quality: User corrections, accepted/rejected suggestions and accuracy monitoring. 
• Audit and economics: Prompt/model trace, access logging, token usage, cost and latency. 
Mandatory Scenarios 
• Material open claim omitted from initial summary 
• Conflicting renewal dates across two systems 
• Apparent cross-sell opportunity lacks sufficient evidence 
• Prompt attempts to expose another client’s restricted information

• 11. Tokenomics and Model Economics Report: Show usage, cost, latency, model rationale, optimisation controls and
